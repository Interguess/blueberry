# Blueberry

Blueberry is a powerful, fully offline-capable voice assistant designed with a focus on privacy, flexibility, and
control. As an open-source project, Blueberry can be self-hosted and easily extended with custom modules, making it
ideal for developers and privacy-conscious users alike.

Whether you're building a local smart home system, crafting custom automations, or just want a voice assistant that
works without relying on the cloud, Blueberry gives you the tools to create your own voice experience — entirely on your
terms.
